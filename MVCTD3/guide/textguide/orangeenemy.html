Orange Enemy:

The Orange Enemy is a step up from the Red Enemy. It teaches the player about Nesting, where enemies have lower tiered ones inside them. More details are listed below.


Description: Value:
Damage needed to fully destroy enemy: 2 (has 1 red enemy inside)
Resistances (if any): None
Total money made by fully destroying enemy: 2
Source image:
Base Speed: 1.25UPF (around 1.3 squares per second)
Official alert text in the game: Orange Enemy:
This is the best time to tell you about an important topic:
NESTING.
In this case, dealing damage to an orange enemy does not kill it, but instead spawns a red enemy in its place.
This will become more prominent during the later rounds, as some enemies may have more than one enemy inside them (hint hint).
